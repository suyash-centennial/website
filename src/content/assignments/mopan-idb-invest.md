## The assignment

The assessment provides a snapshot of the organizational performance of IDB Invest, considering the organization's history, mission, context, trajectory and journey, using the MOPAN methodology. The assessment covers four dimensions of organisational effectiveness: strategic management, examining how effectively long-term objectives are aligned with the mandate and translated into concrete action plans; operational management, gauging how internal processes, structures and resource allocation strategies align with the mission; relationship management, examining the capacity to foster partnerships and collaborations with governments, non-governmental organisations and the private sector; and performance management and results, exploring performance measurement mechanisms and the ability to demonstrate tangible results.

This first MOPAN assessment of IDB Invest following its separation from the IDB was focused on three dimensions, relationship management, performance management and strategic management, to complement a concurrent evaluation by the IDB's Office of Evaluation and Oversight (OVE). Centennial reviewed IDB Invest policies, strategies, knowledge products, appraisal guidelines, safeguards, financials, self-assessments and OVE evaluations, interviewed key staff, and reviewed the portfolios of Brazil, Colombia, El Salvador, Jamaica, Mexico and Uruguay.

## Services provided

The assessment proceeds in four phases: inception, including kick-off and design of the assessment; data collection, including document review, a partner survey, interviews and consultations; report writing, including analysis, quality assurance and finalization; and launch and closing, including launch of the report and lesson learning. Key Performance Indicators and their underlying micro-indicators are assessed against clear judgement criteria.
