## The assignment

The assessment provides a snapshot of the organizational performance of UNEP, considering the organization's history, mission, context, trajectory and journey, using the MOPAN methodology. The assessment covers four dimensions of organisational effectiveness: strategic management, examining how effectively long-term objectives are aligned with the mandate and translated into concrete action plans; operational management, gauging how internal processes, structures and resource allocation strategies align with the mission; relationship management, examining the capacity to foster partnerships and collaborations with governments, non-governmental organisations and the private sector; and performance management and results, exploring performance measurement mechanisms and the ability to demonstrate tangible results.

The assessment applied the MOPAN 3.1 methodology to UNEP's performance from mid-2016 to the end of 2020, a period of transformative shifts in the global environmental discourse.

## Services provided

The assessment proceeds in four phases: inception, including kick-off and design of the assessment; data collection, including document review, a partner survey, interviews and consultations; report writing, including analysis, quality assurance and finalization; and launch and closing, including launch of the report and lesson learning. Key Performance Indicators and their underlying micro-indicators are assessed against clear judgement criteria.
