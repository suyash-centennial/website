## The assignment

The evaluation undertook a real-time assessment and recommended concrete and practical measures for enhancing IsDB Group corporate synergy, with a forward-looking approach. Its outcome was a set of synergy enhancement initiatives, with short- and medium-term quantifiable objectives, key performance indicators and detailed implementation action plans across the IsDB Group and for each of its affiliates. Its recommendations informed the rethinking of the IsDB Group model in light of the President's Five-Year Program.

## Services provided

The evaluation addressed four overarching questions: to what extent and how the implementation of business synergy and processes had been relevant, effective and efficient in delivering development results and better Group harmonization, coordination and partnerships; what enabling and hindering factors had affected the Group's institutional and organizational effectiveness; what the financial value of Group synergy was, in benefits and cost savings, and how to maximize it; and what lessons and recommendations would enhance it. Using a mixed-methods approach, it took stock of internal Group documentation and synergy-related studies, identified landmark institutional and organizational changes and their influence on development outcomes, and examined the relationship between synergy context, mechanisms and outcomes in the performance and governance of the Group's entities.
