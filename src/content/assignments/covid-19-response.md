## The assignment

The assignment evaluated the Bank's COVID-19 response, which aimed to mitigate the impact of the pandemic on African societies and economies by supporting economic and social recovery as well as resilience building. The evaluation assessed the quality, relevance, coherence, efficiency, and effectiveness of the interventions carried out by the Bank and its procedural and process adjustments to meet the urgent needs of Regional Member Countries in the context of the pandemic.

It also assessed the Bank's corporate performance in terms of responsiveness, collaboration, partnership, and institutional set-up, and compared the Bank's instruments and corporate process adaptations with those observed in three other multilateral development banks.

## Services provided

The Centennial team conducted the evaluation in close collaboration with, and under the supervision of, a dedicated IDEV team, employing a selected mix of methods to draw a complete picture of the performance and results of the Bank's COVID-19 response and to provide explanations and causal pathways for those results. The team designed the methods and tools, including questionnaires and project and country sampling, set out the evaluation process and timing, and identified key documents and sources. Country-based team members undertook nine country visits jointly with IDEV. Centennial delivered 14 background reports providing multiple lines of evidence for the Technical Report, on the basis of which IDEV drafted the Summary Evaluation Report.
