## The assignment

The study explores the transformation of the Indian agricultural sector over a 30-year period and proposes policy recommendations for its development: what the sector could look like 30 years from now, and how it should look if it is to meet the needs of an increasingly affluent society.

## Services provided

Drawing on an examination of the previous 30 years of India's agriculture and the key challenges it faces, the study proposed priority recommendations to:

- Make public programs much more focused and effective
- Recognize water as a critical, long-term constraint to agricultural growth and give top priority to improving the efficiency of water use
- Promote new high-yield seeds and related technologies, including mechanization, to improve yields and productivity
- Improve the effectiveness of agricultural research and extension
- Support further improvements in the farm-to-market value chain and reduce spoilage
- Improve markets and incentives through reforms of prices, trade and subsidies
