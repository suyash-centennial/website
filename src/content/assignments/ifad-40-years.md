## The assignment

Written to mark IFAD's 40th anniversary, this knowledge product documents IFAD's operational and thematic evolution since 1977 and, drawing on the challenges its projects and programs confronted and overcame, presents the key lessons from IFAD's experience in agriculture and rural development.

## Services provided

Centennial reviewed IFAD-wide documents, including annual reports, strategic frameworks, effectiveness and impact reports, country strategies, project and grant documents, evaluation reports and policies. In consultation with IFAD's departments and regional divisions, its thematic specialists analyzed a sample of projects illustrating good practice in five areas: agricultural and rural productivity, food security and nutrition; access to services, social inclusion and land rights; gender mainstreaming and women's empowerment; environment and climate change; and rural financial services, institutions and farmers' organizations. Interviews with IFAD staff and reviews of comparator institutions completed the evidence base.
