## The assignment

The study identifies the priority issues that could influence Mexico's long-term economic trajectory, and outlines a balanced action program to address them.

## Services provided

The study focused on the multigenerational issues most likely to determine the country's long-term prospects, used international comparisons to assess Mexico's long-term strengths and weaknesses, and examined the inter-linkages between individual issues. Its proposals were non-ideological, nonpartisan and free of institutional biases.

It set out a long-term framework of seven building blocks from which Mexico's leaders can implement policy reforms, and a prioritized agenda of 18 actions for the next Administration that, implemented together, could bring Mexico out of the middle-income trap and towards a more prosperous and peaceful society.
