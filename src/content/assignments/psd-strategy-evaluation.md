## The assignment

At the request of the Bank's management, IDEV evaluated the 2013–2017 Private Sector Development Strategy to inform the design and implementation of the new strategy.

## Services provided

Centennial undertook background studies and produced reports as inputs to the evaluation of the strategy and its principal interventions.
