## The assignment

Aga Khan University (AKU) commissioned a first-ever study of its economic impact, focusing on its impact in Pakistan. The audiences included AKU's leadership, governments in countries where AKU operates or plans to set up operations, and other potential financiers.

Adopting the standard approach used in economic impact studies of universities, the study measured AKU's economic contribution in gross value added and in jobs, applied to its day-to-day operations and to the private benefits accruing to those who use its services. It also provided a comprehensive analysis of the public benefits AKU generates for third parties and for society at large, including the promotion of equity, which are seldom covered in economic impact studies of universities.

## Services provided

The team reviewed AKU documents, designed and ran student and staff surveys, and undertook an extended mission to Pakistan for further data collection and interviews. It conducted standard economic impact analysis, developed methods to analyze and present qualitative evidence, drafted the main report in consultation with the AKU Steering Committee, and assisted in presenting the report to the Board of Trustees and the Chancellor.
