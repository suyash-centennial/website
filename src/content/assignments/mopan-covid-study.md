## The assignment

An analytical study in MOPAN's Lessons in Multilateral Effectiveness series examining emerging challenges for multilateral organisations in delivering the Sustainable Development Agenda, providing an independent, evidence-based perspective to identify underlying drivers and constraints and offer lessons for strengthening multilateral responses. It identified lessons and policy implications for building on multilateral co-ordination in the response to COVID-19 to address future crises.

The study covered the platforms and mechanisms through which the UN, MDBs and the IMF coordinated their responses, defining co-ordination as voluntary engagement across organisations to share knowledge and evidence, achieve policy and operational coherence, mobilise resources, plan and operate jointly, and monitor progress against collective outcomes. It examined how co-ordination mechanisms contributed to coherence and to country-level preparedness and response, against good practices for effective partnerships including the OECD's ten success factors for effective post-2015 partnerships.

## Services provided

An assessment of multilateral organisations' responses to COVID-19, with a focus on co-ordination mechanisms.
