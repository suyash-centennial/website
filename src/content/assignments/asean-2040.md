## The assignment

The study assessed the performance of the agricultural sector in Indonesia, the Philippines and Vietnam over 1980–2011, its prospects up to 2040, and food security. Its objectives were to:

- Review trends in agricultural production over the past 30 years in the three countries, with particular emphasis on food security policies
- Help the three governments recognize the key constraints they will face in accelerating and sustaining agricultural growth
- Outline strategies to meet the expected increase in food demand up to 2040
- Examine the implications of current and future ASEAN initiatives, particularly on food security, for the agricultural sector of the three countries

## Services provided

Past performance and future scenarios were analyzed with the Centennial Group's Growth Projections Model, adapted to the needs of the agricultural sector. The modeling combined a macroeconomic analysis of future scenarios at the global level and for the three countries with country-level analysis of future total factor productivity, agricultural production and changes in national food consumption. IFPRI's IMPACT model, and for the Philippines the AMPLE model, were used as a countercheck. The team included country experts for Indonesia, the Philippines and Vietnam.
