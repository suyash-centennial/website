## The assignment

The corporate-level evaluation assessed IFAD's efficiency, reviewed past efforts to improve it, and recommended actions to enhance it on a sustainable basis. It was the first evaluation of its kind among multilateral and bilateral aid agencies, and received wide-ranging support from the President of IFAD and the Executive Board.

## Services provided

The evaluation's conceptual framework split IFAD's efficiency into programme efficiency, a measure of how well IFAD deploys its loans and grants to support development outcomes and impacts, including scaled-up impacts that depend on partners' complementary actions; and institutional efficiency, a measure of how well IFAD uses its administrative budget to deliver and manage its programmes, covering the operations complex, oversight and support functions, management of results, people and budget, executive decision-making, and the oversight of the Governing Bodies. It relied on a mix of methods: desk reviews of IFAD's internal documentation; data analysis; evaluations by the Independent Office of Evaluation and audits by the Office of Audit and Oversight; self-evaluations; interviews with staff, managers, country offices, and members of the Board and Evaluation Committee; focus group discussions; and anonymous surveys of Board representatives, staff and managers.
