## The assignment

The study considered investments by Non-Paris Club (NPC) members in Africa, with a focus on China, in light of developments since the outbreak of COVID-19. It linked the analysis to the G20 Compact with Africa initiative and drew policy implications for Germany's bilateral economic policy cooperation in Africa.

The study was implemented through the Emerging Markets Forum, a not-for-profit affiliate of the Centennial Group.

## Services provided

- Analysis of the economic effects of the coronavirus on Ethiopia, Kenya and Zambia
- Updated case studies of the Standard Gauge Railway projects in Ethiopia and Kenya
- Assessment of the risks of over-indebtedness related to the formal and informal deal terms of investments by NPC members
- Assessment of the opportunities for NPC investment in Africa that contributes to sustainable economic growth
- Concepts for trilateral investment, and how synergies with the G20 Compact with Africa could be leveraged
