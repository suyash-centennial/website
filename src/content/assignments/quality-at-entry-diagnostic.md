## The assignment

The diagnostic study informed Senior Management's understanding of the importance of operation quality at entry for both implementation performance and development results; its knowledge of the strengths and weaknesses of the Bank's current practices, tools and processes for assuring quality at entry; and its ability to make evidence-based decisions on future directions and investments to improve quality at entry.

## Services provided

The work began with intensive data collection: a review of AfDB documents, the literature of other MDBs and relevant IDEV evaluations; a review of quality assurance records; and semi-structured interviews with key informants. On this basis the study answered four questions:

- Why does quality at entry matter, and which aspects of quality are the main predictors of implementation performance and development results?
- What are the strengths of the Bank's existing quality at entry practices?
- What are their weaknesses or gaps?
- Is the way the Bank assigns responsibilities and resources for quality at entry optimal, and what are the implications and opportunities of the Development and Business Delivery Model?
