## The assignment

The assessment examined whether the GEF has the right strategy, competitive advantage, organizational architecture, operating framework and level of financing to assist countries in their efforts to achieve their Nationally Determined Contributions, as the GEF approaches its ninth replenishment. It assessed progress in implementing the recommendations of the Seventh Comprehensive Evaluation (OPS-7), reviewed the outcomes and insights of OPS-8, and evaluated the execution of the GEF-8 Strategy and Programming Directions, reflecting on evolving guidance from the global environmental conventions.

## Services provided

A mixed-methods assessment designed to provide evidence on the GEF's effectiveness and capacity to drive transformative action across its mandate in biodiversity, climate change, land degradation, international waters, and chemicals and waste.
