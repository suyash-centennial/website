## The assignment

An analysis of the challenges facing economic growth, equality, public safety, education, competitiveness, and technology and innovation in Latin America, and a strategy to realize a vision of rapid economic growth and faster reduction in disparities over the next three decades.

## Services provided

The study created a long-term vision of Latin American society and economies within which current policy debates and actions can be anchored, and set out the multigenerational issues that countries in the region must tackle to reduce inequities and raise their economic growth rates. It provided analysis and advice on budgetary policy and management, poverty reduction, macroeconomic policy coordination and integration, labor market policy, long-term macroeconomic reforms, innovation and technological development, infrastructure needs, regional cooperation and trade, and governance and political sustainability.

The authors analyzed the reasons for Latin America's underperformance over the previous 30 years and offered an agenda for what national leaders, policy makers and private businesses must do to regain the region's momentum. The report was also translated into Spanish.
