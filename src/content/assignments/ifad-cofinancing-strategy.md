## The assignment

Based on the experience of IFAD and other MDBs with co-financing, the strategy aimed to increase IFAD's impact by raising domestic and international co-financing of IFAD projects to a target ratio of 1.4. It also envisioned improved measurement and monitoring of co-financing, and an action plan to guide IFAD stakeholders in implementing the strategy with domestic and international co-financiers.

## Services provided

Before drafting the strategy, the team reviewed institutional documents on the co-financing experience of IFAD and other IFIs; collected and analyzed data on IFAD's portfolio of co-financed projects to trace the evolution of its relationships with different types of co-financiers; held focus group discussions with IFAD staff on the drivers of, and constraints to, co-financing; reviewed in depth IFAD projects whose co-financing arrangements demonstrate good practice; and designed and conducted a staff survey to identify constraints and the sources of co-financing with the greatest potential to improve project results.
