## The assignment

The African Development Bank established the Middle-Income Countries Technical Assistance Fund (MIC-TAF) in 2002 to enhance the volume, quality, competitiveness and development effectiveness of its operations by providing grant resources to middle-income countries. The evaluation examined the extent to which the MIC-TAF had achieved its original goals and delivered development results in recipient middle-income countries, in accordance with the OECD-DAC evaluation criteria.

## Services provided

A desk review of all relevant documents, including AfDB strategies and policies and the MIC-TAF portfolio from 2002 to 2017; a comprehensive portfolio review of all operations funded by the MIC-TAF since its establishment; case studies of a sample of funded operations; a review of governance arrangements and of practices at comparator organizations, with key lessons and recommendations; and field missions for scoping and data collection, including consultations with Bank staff, government officials, the private sector and civil society organizations in beneficiary countries.
