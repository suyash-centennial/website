## The assignment

Multilateral and bilateral institutions have carried out a large number of evaluations of private sector interventions, including microfinance, private equity, public-private partnerships and SMEs, which contain a wealth of evaluative evidence. The study mined this evaluation knowledge and disseminated it to policy makers in Norway, to the Bank's Board, senior management and staff, to Norad, to key partners including Regional Member Countries and other development institutions, and to the public. It synthesized evaluative evidence across key themes of interest to Norad and the AfDB, covering all evaluations of private sector interventions in Africa by multilateral and bilateral institutions over 2011–2015.

## Services provided

Selection of the evaluations to be analyzed and synthesized; development of thematic and instrument keywords for qualitative data analysis; an in-depth document and literature review; extraction of evaluation findings against the data collection tools; coding and analysis of the data in NVivo against the approved synthesis framework; a report synthesizing and summarizing the findings; and knowledge products, including presentations and videos, to disseminate them.
