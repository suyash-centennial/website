## The assignment

The purpose was to develop a long-term strategy that positions CAF to become a leader and trusted partner in Latin America's overall economic development and related policy issues.

CAF's success, particularly since 2010, underpinned the need for formulating a long-term corporate strategy. CAF had completed its transformation from a modest-sized, sub-regional lender focused on the Andean region to the leading, full-fledged Pan-American development institution. In addition to its leadership in lending for infrastructure development and financial intermediation, CAF is also more proactive in facilitating regional cooperation.

The new strategy was motivated, among other factors, by the need to take account of the accelerating pace of change in the structure and evolution of the global economy and the underlying mega-trends that will likely have a major impact on the long-term evolution of Latin America: the accelerating pace of globalization; the rise of emerging markets, particularly in Asia; increasing competition for natural resources; innovation and technological advances; the increasing adverse impact of climate change; and a narrowing time window during which Latin America will still enjoy a demographic dividend. The strategy also aimed to incorporate economic growth, inclusion, and environmental sustainability in a systematic manner in CAF's future activities.

## Services provided

In support of the formulation of CAF's long-term strategy, Centennial researched, wrote and produced eleven Background Papers on topics such as regional integration, long-term growth prospects for the region, global value chains, indicators of business environment, microeconomic efficiency, commodity terms of trade in emerging markets, and the economic history of Latin America as well as the history of CAF.

Centennial also produced one cohesive, long-term corporate strategy for CAF through 2030, which it discussed with and presented to the CAF Board of Directors on multiple occasions.
