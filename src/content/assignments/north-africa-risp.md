## The assignment

The Mid-Term Evaluation of the North Africa Regional Integration Strategy Paper (RISP) 2020–2026 was a formative evaluation designed to inform the Board and stakeholders about the progress made in implementing the strategy and to recommend corrective actions to enhance the achievement of development outcomes. It assessed the delivery of expected outputs and results against the strategy's Results Measurement Framework, identified factors that enabled or hindered progress, and drew lessons to guide the remainder of the implementation period.

The evaluation focused on the Indicative Operations Program and covered all Bank-approved operations from May 2020 to December 2023, examining their relevance, alignment with regional integration goals, coherence with other interventions, and sustainability. Key evaluation questions addressed the strategy's effectiveness, organizational readiness, consideration of fragility, and the need for strategic adjustments.

## Services provided

Preparation of an inception report outlining the evaluation methodology, timeline and team roles, and development of an evaluation matrix; an extensive review of relevant documentation; data collection missions across countries in the region; individual project sheets, interview notes and sector-specific technical analyses; the Technical Report, with analysis, key observations, findings, lessons learned and recommendations; presentations summarizing key findings; and written responses to feedback from peer reviewers and the Evaluation Reference Group, with participation in Reference Group meetings.
