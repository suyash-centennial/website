## The assignment

The team reviewed the activities of the African Development Bank and produced cohesive annual reports for each year from 2018 to 2023, paying particular attention to progress on the High 5 priorities and to regional achievements in approvals and portfolio management.

## Services provided

A broad analysis of the Bank's performance against key metrics including approvals and disbursements; a review of the activities and performance of Bank units; compilation and analysis of approval data to assess progress on the High 5s; a review of the Bank's financial data, including its rating and risk management measures; and identification and interpretation of global, regional and sub-regional political and economic trends and their implications for the Bank.
