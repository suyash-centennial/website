## The assignment

Each assessment of a multilateral organisation is conducted using the MOPAN methodology and aims to capture a panoramic overview of the organisation's performance. The assessment covers four dimensions of organisational effectiveness: strategic management, examining how effectively long-term objectives are aligned with the mandate and translated into concrete action plans; operational management, gauging how internal processes, structures and resource allocation strategies align with the mission; relationship management, examining the capacity to foster partnerships and collaborations with governments, non-governmental organisations and the private sector; and performance management and results, exploring performance measurement mechanisms and the ability to demonstrate tangible results. Each organisation's evolution is examined within the context of its historical development, core mission, prevailing dynamics, and trajectory.

## Services provided

The assessment is conducted in four phases: inception, kick-off and design of the assessment; data collection, including document review, a partner survey, interviews and consultations; analysis, report drafting, quality assurance and finalization; and launch of the report and lesson learning.
