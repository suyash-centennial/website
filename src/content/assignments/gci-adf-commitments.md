## The assignment

IDEV retained Centennial to carry out an evaluation of the Bank's commitment process and the implementation of commitments related to its Sixth General Capital Increase (GCI-VI) and the 12th and 13th Replenishments of the African Development Fund (ADF-12 and ADF-13). The second part of the assignment evaluated the Bank's policy and strategy suite, to support the Bank's effectiveness by helping it improve the role of its policies and strategies.

## Services provided

The first part of the evaluation aimed to improve how Governors, Deputies and the Bank manage GCI and ADF commitments. It assessed the efficiency of the GCI and ADF replenishment processes, evaluated the relevance of the portfolio of commitments in terms of the Bank's strategic priorities, validated the delivery of commitments, and evaluated the effectiveness of their implementation. The second part assessed the relevance of the Bank's active policy and strategy suite, the efficiency of its processes for formulating policies and strategies, and their effectiveness in guiding the Bank's work. Centennial undertook a document and literature review, an analysis of all policies and strategies approved since 2009, case studies of 11 policies and strategies, key informant interviews, focus group discussions, a staff survey, and an analysis of benchmarking data from comparator institutions.
