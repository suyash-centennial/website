## The assignment

The evaluation assessed the AsDB's performance in agriculture in a changing context, with a focus on four case-study countries. It had three components: a strategic review, a portfolio review and an institutional review, including the review of 110 Project Completion Reports and their validation reports to evaluate performance, identify contributing factors and distill lessons.

## Services provided

- A corporate and country strategy review of AsDB policies, strategies, country partnership strategies, knowledge products, self-assessments and IED evaluations relating to agriculture
- A review of completed projects, the ongoing portfolio and the pipeline in agriculture and natural resources, across subsectors and countries
- An assessment of 110 Project Completion Reports and about 90 validation reports
- Country missions and country working papers for Bangladesh, Cambodia, China and Tajikistan
- Key informant interviews and focus group discussions at AsDB headquarters, and a review of other multilateral financial institutions' support to agriculture
