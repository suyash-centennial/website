## The assignment

Centennial organized three events to discuss and update the Africa 2050 policy papers:

- A JICA Seminar at the TICAD VI Summit in Nairobi
- A symposium on the revised policy papers on infrastructure and regional integration at the 12th Infrastructure Consortium for Africa Annual Meetings in Abidjan
- An Africa Emerging Markets Forum in Abidjan, chaired by President Ouattara

## Services provided

Centennial assisted JICA staff and management with the design of the TICAD VI Seminar, identified and invited panel members, prepared a brochure summarizing the policy papers, and presented the papers at the Seminar.

For the ICA symposium, Centennial supported the design of the overall symposium and its sessions, prepared briefing notes, delivered the opening presentation and wrote the post-symposium summary report. Its experts also produced the background paper, which reviewed the history and accomplishments of the ICA and the contributions of multilateral and bilateral donors, particularly the initiatives of the AfDB.

Centennial led the design, management and organization of the Africa Emerging Markets Forum. In advance of the Forum, it updated the policy papers to reflect discussants' comments and changes in the global economy, and launched them as the book Africa Reset: A New Way Forward. Centennial staff also presented at the Forum on infrastructure development and on Africa's long-term economic and social outlook.
