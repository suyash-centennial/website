## The assignment

The project had two phases: a retrospective evaluation of the implementation of BOAD's 2015–2019 strategic plan, with identification of potential priorities for the next plan; and preparation of the 2021–2025 strategic plan.

## Services provided

- Interviews with key BOAD staff and Board members, and document reviews
- Benchmarking of BOAD's performance against comparators
- A full evaluation of the previous strategic plan
- Elaboration with BOAD management of priorities for the next five-year plan, validated by BOAD's Board
- Joint preparation with BOAD management of the 2021–2025 plan, including implementation milestones and monitoring indicators
