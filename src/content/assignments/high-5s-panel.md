## The assignment

Centennial supported, technically and logistically, a High-Level Panel co-chaired by Kofi Annan and Horst Köhler and composed of former Heads of State, heads of international organizations, former Cabinet-level ministers, private sector leaders, and academics, convened to advise the AfDB on achieving its development goals under the High 5s. The Panel's report addressed the High 5 priorities and how they could best be achieved and financed, and included recommendations on improving the Bank's operations and organization.

## Services provided

Centennial produced a series of background papers and notes on the High 5 priorities and related strategies, the Bank's Development and Business Delivery Model, and other development issues including illicit capital flows, development financing, and the global and regional economic context. It produced the Report of the Panel, presenting the Panel's findings and recommendations, and planned and supported the Panel's three meetings, in Berlin (October 2016), Zurich (January 2017) and Abidjan (March 2017).
