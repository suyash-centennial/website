## The assignment

The study assessed the many challenges Africa faces in becoming a prosperous continent, economically, socially and politically, and offered an ambitious strategy for realizing economic prosperity, equality, modernization, public safety, a stronger voice in global governance, economic diversification and a reduction in unemployment.

The findings were presented at the Emerging Markets Forum in Côte d'Ivoire and at the TICAD V Summit in Tokyo in June 2013, and at the Governors' Forum of the AfDB Annual Meetings in Kigali in May 2014.

## Services provided

The Centennial Group compiled an analytical report covering all sectors, including macroeconomics, poverty, demography, urbanization, and natural resources and their relation to sustainable and inclusive growth in Africa. The report considered how emerging megatrends, such as the shift to a multipolar world, demographic transitions, climate change and digitization, will influence Africa's prospects, and assessed the prospects for regional integration in a way that boosts peace, security and competitiveness.

Using the Centennial Group's econometric model of the global economy, the team developed three scenarios for future growth in Africa: convergence, business as usual, and downside. The model projects annual real GDP growth through 2050 as a function of labor force, capital stock and total factor productivity.
