## The assignment

As part of a program advising the National Development Fund of Saudi Arabia on the implementation of its long-term strategy, Centennial prepared a pre-feasibility study for the creation of an infrastructure fund for Saudi Arabia.

## Services provided

Centennial assessed the justification for a national infrastructure fund and carried out the modeling, financial analysis and organizational design, and identified an initial pipeline of infrastructure projects in renewable energy, transportation, water supply and other sectors.
