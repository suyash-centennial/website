## The assignment

As part of its 2019 work program, IDEV evaluated the Bank's portfolio review and restructuring policy and its subsequent guidelines.

## Services provided

The team carried out a desk review and in-country work on the effectiveness of country portfolio performance review strategies, and distilled findings and lessons from the implementation of the Bank's 1995 policy on portfolio review and restructuring, the 2011 guidelines on the portfolio review process, and the guidelines on project cancellation issued in 2011 for public sector operations and in 2013 for private sector operations.
