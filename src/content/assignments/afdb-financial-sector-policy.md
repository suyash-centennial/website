## The assignment

The project entailed the finalization of the Bank's Financial Sector Development Policy and Strategy, 2014–2019.

## Services provided

Centennial worked intensively with the Director of the Financial Sector Development Department and the task manager for the Policy and Strategy to formulate a vision for the sector, the pillars of the Bank's policy and strategy with the underpinning activities and instruments, and an implementation plan. This included preparing presentations for the Director for review meetings, the Senior Management Coordination Committee, the Board's Committee on Operations and Development Effectiveness, and the Annual Meetings in Kigali.
