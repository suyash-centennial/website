## The assignment

The study analyzes economic prospects for Central Asia through 2050, examining trade, human development, institutions, the environment, agriculture and energy.

## Services provided

The authors identified the region's key challenges: the efficient development of the energy, agriculture, industry and services sectors; inclusive human development; adaptation to climate change; economic integration with global and regional markets; and improvements in governance and institutions. A challenge cutting across all others is how Central Asia manages its increasingly scarce and critical water resources.

The study explored alternative scenarios of economic and social development in the region to 2050, depending on how well the region contends with these challenges. The scenarios were derived from the Centennial Group's in-house models, drawing on hundreds of economic, social, financial and governance indicators.
