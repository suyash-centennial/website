## The assignment

At the request of the GEF CEO, Centennial undertook a restructuring exercise to optimize the GEF Secretariat's organizational structure and operational procedures, enhancing its capacity to execute its broader mandate within the GEF Partnership given the heightened demands of the eighth replenishment cycle (2023–2026) and the Kunming-Montréal biodiversity outcomes.

The review covered the Secretariat's internal structure and the responsibilities of its units and teams; a "one GEF" approach to overcome siloed operations; avenues for staff development aligned with the organization's needs; strategic staffing, planning, budgeting, and portfolio and risk management; and a pilot decentralization program that would bring selected Secretariat roles and functions closer to recipient countries.

## Services provided

The team conducted document analysis and internal data analysis, and interviewed more than 80 percent of Secretariat staff and managers, key members of the GEF Council, the GEF Independent Evaluation Office, and representatives of 18 Implementing Agencies, and took part in the Inter-Agency meeting in Rome in March 2023. Interim and final reports, refined through consultations with the CEO and his principal advisor, analyzed workflows and human resources and set out organizational principles and potential structures. The final report was presented to the GEF Council in Brasília in June 2023.
