## The assignment

The study assessed the economic and social impact of infrastructure investments financed by Non-Paris Club (NPC) countries in Cameroon, Ethiopia, Ghana, Kenya and Zambia. It followed an "inside-out" approach that builds on local expertise as a major source of the research, with country experts in each of the five countries.

The study reviewed three major infrastructure projects to compare projects financed by NPC and Paris Club sources, and assessed the impact of overall NPC lending to the five countries on their debt sustainability.

## Services provided

The team prepared five country studies, five project case studies and a synthesis report, and conducted a survey on debt sustainability focusing on debt from NPC lenders at both the macroeconomic and the project level.
