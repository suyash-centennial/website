## The assignment

The Quality Assurance Implementation Plan set out a 10-point plan to enhance the quality and developmental impact of the African Development Bank's operations. It presents the operational details for implementing these actions, with a clear and prioritized sequence of time-bound deliverables covering the short to medium term.

## Services provided

The Centennial team assisted Bank Management in elaborating and validating the plan, providing expertise on operations management that led to the identification of key priority areas for action, and played a central role in preparing the resulting 10-Point Plan to Raise the Bar on Quality and Results.
