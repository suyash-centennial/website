## The assignment

Contribution to the development of the AfDB Long-Term Strategy as a member of the high-level technical panel.

## Services provided

Assistance in developing a strategy for the African Development Bank for the next decade, grounded in a long-term vision for Africa, with a focus on change in the operational forces of the Bank and on its institutional reform, and on institutional effectiveness in delivering impact in client countries.
