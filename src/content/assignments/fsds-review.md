## The assignment

The Financial Sector Development Strategy 2014–2019 was anchored in the vision of the Bank Group's Ten-Year Strategy 2013–2022. In September 2015 the Bank adopted an agenda to accelerate implementation of the Ten-Year Strategy through five high-priority areas, the High 5s. The formative review assessed progress in implementing the strategy in light of its objectives, focusing on relevance, effectiveness and efficiency, as it was too soon to assess impact and sustainability. Drawing on its results and on changes in the financial and private sector landscape in Africa and globally, the review also framed strategic choices for the future.

## Services provided

The review assessed the relevance of the strategy's vision and objectives and of the design of the overall financial sector program; evaluated the effectiveness and efficiency of the Financial Sector Development Department's activities in implementing the strategy; assessed the alignment of activities with the strategy's results framework and the adequacy of its M&E systems to detect needed adjustments; identified how the financial sector results framework could be aligned with the Ten-Year Strategy and the High 5s; made recommendations on accelerating implementation of the strategy and its successor; and proposed an action plan for formulating the new strategy for 2020–2025.
