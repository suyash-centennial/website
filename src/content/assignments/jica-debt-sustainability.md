## The assignment

The program provided detailed macroeconomic analysis covering the real sector, industrial structure, inflation, monetary and exchange rate policy, the banking sector, government budgets and state-owned enterprises, public debt, the balance of payments and external debt. It also provided medium- to long-term macroeconomic outlooks, of 5 to 20 years, based on financial programming and debt sustainability analysis, taking into account the international economic crisis.

Over the period, the team carried out debt sustainability analysis for more than 20 countries, including Bangladesh, Bosnia, Brazil, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Egypt, Ethiopia, Georgia, Ghana, Kenya, Madagascar, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nigeria, Rwanda, Senegal, South Africa, Tunisia, Turkey, Uganda, Ukraine and Zambia.

## Services provided

The team, directed by a former Director of the IMF, completed missions to each country and met senior officials of government ministries and agencies, and institutions such as rating agencies, commercial banks and research institutions. Drawing on the results of these missions, it delivered a week-long training seminar on financial programming and debt sustainability analysis to help JICA staff strengthen their analytical capacity in sovereign risk analysis.
