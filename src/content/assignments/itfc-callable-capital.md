## The assignment

Centennial analyzed alternatives for increasing ITFC's callable capital and prepared the business case for the selected alternative, including a review of ITFC's Articles of Agreement, policies and rules to identify constraints, and a review of the approaches of other MDBs.

## Services provided

**Case for callable capital**: a review of ITFC's plans and their implications for financing and callable capital; an analysis of the constraints imposed by its Articles of Agreement and internal rules; and preparation of the Board paper on additional callable capital and a presentation for the Board.

**Resource mobilization**: a review of ITFC's approaches to resource mobilization, compared with those of the African Development Bank, the International Finance Corporation, the Clean Technology Fund, CAF and the World Bank; lessons and recommendations for ITFC; and alternative forms of resource mobilization, including shareholder guarantees, sukuk issues, loan sales, securitizations, new trust funds and hybrid capital, with their implications for ITFC's credit rating.
