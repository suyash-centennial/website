## The assignment

In 2013 the Bank adopted its Ten-Year Strategy 2013–2022. Nearly five years into its implementation, the AfDB carried out a mid-term review combining a retrospective assessment of progress with a forward look at how the realization of the strategy's objectives could be accelerated in the 2018–2022 period. The review began by assessing the relevance of the strategy's vision and objectives when it was formulated and in light of subsequent internal and external developments. With this as context, it addressed three overarching questions relating to the three planks of the strategy: the Bank's strategic approach and priorities, financing the strategy by mobilizing and leveraging resources, and transforming the Bank to support Africa's transformation.

## Services provided

The review assessed progress against each of the strategy's twin objectives, core priority areas and cross-cutting issues, and toward implementing the High 5s, distilling challenges, opportunities and lessons. It analyzed how the Bank had allocated its risk capital by priority, between sovereign and non-sovereign operations, and among financing instruments, toward a strategy for optimizing allocations for impact, including new instruments. It assessed the extent to which Bank activities focused on results and whether its monitoring, evaluation and learning systems could drive mid-course adjustments. It formulated recommendations on accelerating implementation through policies and strategies, pipeline quality, portfolio restructuring, institutional reforms including consolidation of the Development and Business Delivery Model, and capacity building, together with an action plan for the remainder of the strategy period.
