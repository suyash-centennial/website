## The assignment

The purpose of the evaluation was twofold: to assess prospects for progress in achieving development results in the context of the Regional Integration Strategy Paper; and to suggest lessons and potential improvements to feed into and help guide the preparation of the next regional integration strategy.

## Services provided

The evaluation covered the strategy and the related portfolio of 24 regional operations in East Africa approved between 2011 and 2015. Its findings were based on triangulation across three approaches: a literature and document review supplemented with interviews; a portfolio review; and in-depth, field-based project results assessments. It evaluated the relevance, effectiveness, sustainability, and coherence of the Bank's contributions to regional integration in Eastern Africa, as well as the design and delivery, efficiency, and donor coordination of the Bank's management of regional interventions, and made six recommendations for the Bank's future regional integration operations.
