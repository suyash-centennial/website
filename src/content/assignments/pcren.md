## The assignment

A team of expert evaluators assessed the quality and validated the performance of more than 100 AfDB projects. On the basis of this review, the team prepared Project Completion Report Evaluation Notes (PCRENs) that validated the lessons and recommendations stated in the projects' completion reports and evaluated the quality of those reports. The team leader summarized the findings in a synthesis report on completion report quality, project monitoring and evaluation systems, and underlying project performance, lessons, and recommendations, and prepared a brief on issues identified in the review process and template.

## Services provided

For each project, the evaluators reviewed all available relevant documents, including project completion reports, appraisal reports, loan agreements, country strategy papers, tranche release documents, quality-at-entry and readiness review reports, supervision and back-to-office reports, mid-term reviews and restructuring documents, implementation progress reports, and audit reports. On this basis they drafted a PCREN for each project assessing the relevance, gender sensitivity and effectiveness of results and M&E systems, the validity of the completion report ratings, the quality of each report, and the factors supporting or limiting its quality. The synthesis report set out recommendations for improving report quality, measures for strengthening project performance, and lessons considered at sector level and through cross-cutting themes such as capacity building, gender, and safeguards.
