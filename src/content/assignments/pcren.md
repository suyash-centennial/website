## The assignment

Across successive rounds covering 2017, 2018 and 2019 operations, teams of expert evaluators assessed the quality and validated the performance of more than 100 AfDB projects. On the basis of this review, the teams prepared Project Completion Report Evaluation Notes (PCRENs) that validated the lessons and recommendations stated in the projects' completion reports and evaluated the quality of those reports.

The team leader summarized the findings in synthesis reports on completion report quality, project monitoring and evaluation systems, including gender sensitivity, and underlying project performance, lessons and recommendations, and prepared a brief on issues identified in the review process and template, with suggestions on how the process could be strengthened.

## Services provided

For each project, the evaluators reviewed all available relevant documents, including project completion reports, appraisal reports, loan agreements, country strategy papers, tranche release documents, quality-at-entry and readiness review reports, supervision and back-to-office reports, mid-term reviews and restructuring documents, implementation progress reports, and audit reports. On this basis they drafted a PCREN for each project assessing the extent to which the project's results and M&E systems were relevant, gender-sensitive and effective; the validity of the completion report ratings; the quality of each report; and the factors supporting or limiting its quality. The synthesis reports set out recommendations for improving report quality, measures for strengthening project performance, and lessons considered at sector level and through cross-cutting themes such as capacity building, gender, and safeguards.
