## The assignment

A sector-wide evaluation of the AsDB's energy policy and program, with its increasing emphasis on renewable energy and climate change.

## Services provided

The evaluation assessed and informed the AsDB Board of Directors and Management on the AsDB's experience in supporting the energy sector in developing member countries during the study period, and provided guidance on further engagement in the future.
