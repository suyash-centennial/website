## The assignment

Africa Reset compiles a suite of policy papers on the economic, social and political challenges facing Africa, first presented at the TICAD VI Summit in Nairobi and finalized at a meeting of the Africa Emerging Markets Forum in Abidjan chaired by President Ouattara. It updates the vision of Africa's long-term development set out in its predecessor, Africa 2050, in light of rising global economic headwinds and mixed developments on the international scene, and offers a new action plan for realizing that vision.

The plan includes strategies to reduce African countries' vulnerability to commodity cycles, accelerate the diversification of their economies, create jobs while reducing inequality, promote and reap the benefits of regional economic integration, and bridge Africa's infrastructure gaps in a sustainable manner.

## Services provided

The team collected, collated and analyzed economic, financial and social data from reputable sources, supplemented with information collected from original sources, and reviewed relevant studies and their proposals. On the basis of this research, it carried out extensive modeling with the Centennial Group's in-house model of the global economy to project annual real GDP growth through 2050 as a function of labor force, capital stock and total factor productivity.
