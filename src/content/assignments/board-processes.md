## The assignment

The study presented a synthesis of experiences and lessons the AfDB could learn from other international organizations regarding Board processes, procedures and practices, and the extent to which they contribute to the effectiveness and efficiency of the Board of Executive Directors in fulfilling its mandate. It was designed as a fact-finding, comparative study leading to the identification of lessons and good practices.

## Services provided

The study covered corporate governance, accountability, Board operations, and support to the Board, including the Board's interface with the Board of Governors, its relationship with the President and senior management, and the role of the Secretariat. It was grounded in a systematic review of Board-related documents, literature and past assessments, with comparators including the World Bank, IMF, Inter-American Development Bank, CAF, EBRD, New Development Bank, Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank, Global Fund and Asian Development Bank, and drew on interviews with Board members, secretariat staff, and Presidents and senior managers.
