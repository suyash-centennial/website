## The assignment

Centennial supported the IsDB in analyzing the potential impact of a capital increase on the Bank's approval capacity and scale of operations, and in developing an implementation plan.

## Services provided

- Preparation of the Technical Report on the capital increase, with messages accessible to technical and non-technical audiences
- Peer review by senior experts familiar with MDB mandates and capital increase matters on the Bank's business case and narrative
- Guidance on scenarios to determine the size and payment schedule of the capital increase, and on stress scenarios run by the IsDB team
- Articulation of the IsDB's value proposition in return for the capital increase, in line with other MDBs
- Updating of the Technical Report for the Board of Governors, and advice on executive briefs for Board members and Governors
