## The assignment

A key objective of the study was to identify the prerequisites for Kazakhstan's vision of joining the ranks of the world's top 30 economies by 2050.

## Services provided

Based on a review of Kazakhstan's socioeconomic development over the previous 20 years and an analysis of the outlook for the global economy, the authors explored how Kazakhstan can build the foundations for an inclusive modern society in seven priority areas:

- Building a strong human resource base
- Managing its energy resources sustainably
- Growing a green economy
- Pursuing balanced and efficient urban and regional development
- Creating a diversified, modern knowledge economy
- Becoming fully integrated with the rest of the world
- Underpinning all else, building effective and inclusive institutions
