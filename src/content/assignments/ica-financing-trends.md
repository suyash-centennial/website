## The assignment

Centennial prepared the Infrastructure Consortium for Africa's Infrastructure Financing Trends reports for 2018 and 2019–2020, collecting infrastructure financing information from international and regional sources and interviewing stakeholders to report on the trends in, and funding of, infrastructure projects in Africa.

## Services provided

The team collected financing data from development banks and other multilateral and bilateral actors and distilled it to show trends in commitments and disbursements by country, funding instrument and sector, supplemented by interviews with stakeholders and funders on trends and future plans. It prepared special reports on urban infrastructure financing and quality infrastructure financing. The 2019–2020 report also assessed the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic and the importance of resilience, addressed financial sustainability as a condition of quality infrastructure, examined newly emerging institutional models such as the Caisses des Dépôts established in Morocco and Tunisia, and analyzed elements of the energy transition in Africa.
