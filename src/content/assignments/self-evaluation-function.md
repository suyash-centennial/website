## The assignment

The evaluation assessed how well the Bank's self-evaluation systems and processes perform, focusing on their relevance, coherence, efficiency, effectiveness and short-term impact; identified the enablers and barriers that affect their design, implementation and results; and distilled lessons and good practices to help the Bank enhance their quality and performance.

Its findings were designed to support the Bank in improving self-evaluation and the performance management of operations, strategies and policies; improving the relevance and quality of the Bank's Operations Manual; promoting learning from experience; supporting implementation of the Development and Business Delivery Model; and accounting to the Board of Directors and other stakeholders for the results of investments in self-evaluation.

## Services provided

The team reviewed documents from the AfDB and comparator organizations on the evolution of self-evaluation systems, followed by a meta-analysis of how MDBs assess their self-evaluation systems with respect to performance management, accountability and learning, and benchmarked the components of MDBs' self-evaluation systems. It carried out key informant interviews and surveys of Executive Directors, managers and staff, and case studies of a representative sample of closed projects, producing five background papers and a final evaluation report.
