## The assignment

An analytical study in MOPAN's Lessons in Multilateral Effectiveness series examining how multilateral organisations, including MDBs, UN organisations, the IMF, the Green Climate Fund and the Global Environment Facility, have responded to climate change. The study was prepared to inform preparations for the 26th UN Climate Change Conference of the Parties (COP26) and to support stakeholders in engaging with multilateral organisations at the conference.

## Services provided

A document review, a survey conducted with MOPAN, and interviews with key stakeholders, followed by an extensive phase of analysis, the synthesis of lessons, and the formulation of recommendations to inform COP26 discussions in Glasgow.
