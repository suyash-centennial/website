## The assignment

The overall objective was to contribute to building a long-term vision and to formulate a development strategy for Nigerien society and economy to 2035, equipping Niger with a long-term vision of its development and a program of reforms.

## Services provided

- Collection, processing, analysis and management of databases, including through the design and implementation of surveys
- Retrospective analysis of the current situation
- Identification of key challenges for the policy support program
- Development of technical tools for scenario construction, the vision and the development strategy for Niger to 2035
- Development of the Niger Vision 2035
- Proposals for long-term development strategies and supporting policies
- Building the human resource capacity of the Ministry of Planning in strategic planning
- Seminars and workshops to present and validate the studies and the vision
