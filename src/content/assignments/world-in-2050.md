## The assignment

The study takes a long-term perspective on the economic and social outlook of the world to 2050, with a main focus on today's emerging and developing economies, and on the cross-cutting, intergenerational issues that often get overshadowed by short-term crises and political preoccupations.

## Services provided

The book was a joint effort of a multidisciplinary team of 26 authors born in twelve countries on five continents. It looks back at key economic and social developments over the past half century, then addresses whether the era of rapid convergence is over and whether emerging economies are destined to be caught in the middle-income trap, or can resume their march towards rising living standards through technological development and productivity gains. Much of the book concentrates on the cross-cutting and intergenerational issues that will determine what kind of world future generations will inherit.
