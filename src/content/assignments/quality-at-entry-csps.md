## The assignment

The assignment carried out the independent quality-at-entry assessment of the Bank's Country Strategy Papers, including full Country Strategy Papers, Interim Strategy Papers and Joint Country Assistance Strategy Papers, and of its Regional Integration Strategy Papers.

## Services provided

The evaluation had two purposes. First, it assessed the quality at entry of the strategy papers and whether quality had improved since the previous independent assessment of papers approved in 2008 and 2009 (retrospective component). Second, it suggested improvements to the Bank's design process for country and regional integration strategies in light of the Ten-Year Strategy 2013–2022 (prospective component).
