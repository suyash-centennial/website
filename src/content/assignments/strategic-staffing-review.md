## The assignment

The review examined staffing levels and mix at the AfDB and benchmarked them against comparator institutions. From this information, and a review of the Bank's work program and desired quality outcomes, a comprehensive projection of required staffing was derived from a model developed for the assignment, taking account of opportunities to improve efficiency and productivity. The projection and its implications were discussed in a report to Bank Management, the Committee on Administrative Affairs and Human Resources Policy Issues, and the Board. The team also reviewed the human resources policies relating to staffing and staff development.

## Services provided

A review of historical staffing levels and mix and of staff time utilization; benchmarking of staffing, budget and time use against comparator organizations; identification of efficiency gains and measures to improve productivity; a bespoke model to project staffing needs and mix from future work plans; a review and benchmarking of the Bank's use of consultants and their effect on quality and cost; recommendations on HR policies to support strategic staff planning, including staff mobility, types of appointment, and talent boards; and participation in Management and Board meetings on proposed staffing changes and their budget and quality implications.
