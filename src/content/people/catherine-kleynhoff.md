Ms. Catherine Kleynhoff is a senior evaluation and development finance expert with more than 30 years of experience at the World Bank and as an advisor to multilateral development institutions. Her expertise spans finance, private sector development, infrastructure, education, and monitoring and evaluation, with particularly extensive experience in Africa.

Since joining Centennial, she has served as Senior Expert on the Mid-Term Evaluation of the North Africa Regional Integration Strategy Paper 2020–2026, contributed to the Review of the AfDB Financial Sector Development Strategy 2014–2019, and completed more than 20 Project Completion Report Evaluation Notes for the Bank in French, most concerning financial sector operations. She was responsible for the chapter analyzing Bank Group operations through the High 5 priorities in six consecutive AfDB Annual Reports, 2018–2023, contributed to the Infrastructure Consortium for Africa's infrastructure financing trends reports, and co-authored the IFAD Co-financing Strategy.

During her World Bank career, she worked across Africa, the Middle East and North Africa, and Central Asia on financial and private sector development, institutional performance, operational quality, and learning. She has lectured on development finance at Sciences Po, Paris.
