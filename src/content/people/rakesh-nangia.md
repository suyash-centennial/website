Mr. Rakesh Nangia is the Chief Executive Officer of Centennial Advisory. Prior to founding Centennial Advisory, he was Partner and Chief Operating Officer of the Centennial Group.

He previously served as Evaluator General for Independent Development Evaluation (IDEV) at the African Development Bank, where he led the Bank's independent evaluation function. Before joining the AfDB, he spent 25 years at the World Bank, where his positions included Director of Strategy and Operations for the Human Development Network and Acting Vice-President for the World Bank Institute.

He led the preparation of IFAD's evaluation policy and strategy, and has served on Centennial's MOPAN assessment teams. His expertise spans independent evaluation, program and project design and management, and institutional effectiveness. He holds degrees in engineering and business administration.
