Mr. Rakesh Nangia is the Chief Executive Officer of Centennial Advisory. Prior to founding Centennial Advisory, he was Partner and Chief Executive Officer of the Centennial Group.

He previously served as Evaluator General for Independent Development Evaluation (IDEV) at the African Development Bank, where he led the Bank's independent evaluation function. Before joining the AfDB, he spent 25 years at the World Bank, where his positions included Director of Strategy and Operations for the Human Development Network and Acting Vice-President for the World Bank Institute.

With Centennial, he has served as Team Leader and Project Director of the MOPAN assessments of the World Bank, IFAD and IFC, Project Director of the MOPAN assessment of UNEP, and Engagement Director of the MOPAN focused assessment of IDB Invest. He led the Corporate Evaluation of IsDB Group Synergy for the Islamic Development Bank Group. He also led the preparation of IFAD's evaluation policy and strategy. His expertise spans independent evaluation, program and project design and management, and institutional effectiveness. He holds degrees in engineering and business administration.
