Mr. Anil Sood is a senior development strategy and evaluation expert with more than 45 years of experience in multilateral development, institutional strategy, governance, and organizational reform.

During nearly 30 years at the World Bank, he held senior leadership positions including Vice President for Strategy and Resource Management, Special Advisor to the Managing Director, Director of Regional Operations for Europe and Central Asia, and Director of the Technical Department for Europe and Central Asia, the Middle East and North Africa. As Director of Regional Operations, he managed development operations across all sectors and countries of the region, overseeing approximately 1,000 staff and annual lending of approximately USD 5 billion. He led a Bank-wide renewal initiative that reorganized more than 3,000 professional staff into four networks.

Since leaving the World Bank, he has advised the leadership of numerous multilateral development institutions and led major strategic, institutional, and independent evaluation assignments for the African Development Bank (AfDB), Asian Development Bank (AsDB), International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD), Islamic Development Bank (IsDB), and MOPAN. He was Advisor to the President of the AfDB from 2005 to 2008.

His AfDB work includes co-leading the independent evaluation of the AfDB's response to COVID-19 for IDEV, senior oversight of the IDEV Evaluation of the Middle-Income Countries Technical Assistance Fund, leading the Mid-Term Review of the AfDB Ten-Year Strategy 2013–2022, the Strategic Staffing Review, and the AfDB Annual Reports 2018–2023. For other institutions, he led the Corporate-Level Evaluation of IFAD Efficiency, the first evaluation of its kind among multilateral and bilateral agencies, and the Independent Assessment of the Technical Capacity of FAO, and co-led MOPAN's analytical study of the multilateral response to COVID-19.

He brings extensive first-hand understanding of MDB governance, strategy, organizational reform, development finance, and institutional performance, complemented by substantial experience across Africa, Asia, Europe and Central Asia, and the Middle East.
