Dr. Albert-Eneas Gakusi is a development evaluation expert who served as Chief Evaluation Officer at Independent Development Evaluation (IDEV), the African Development Bank's independent evaluation function, until December 2020.

At the AfDB he led evaluations including *Fostering Inclusive Finance in Africa: An Evaluation of the Bank's Microfinance Policy, Strategy and Operations, 2000–2012*, and the Bank's evaluation of its assistance to regional integration and multinational operations. His published work covers regional integration and multinational projects in Africa, the design and performance of lines of credit, the credibility and independence of development evaluation, education policy, and public policy and health outcomes in sub-Saharan Africa.

He is the author of *Answers to Frequently Asked Questions on Development Evaluation* (2026), and works in English and French.
