Mr. Gaiv Tata is a senior development finance expert with more than 35 years of experience in international development, specializing in private and financial sector development, aid architecture, and international development finance.

During more than 25 years at the World Bank, he served as Director for Private Sector Development and Finance for Sub-Saharan Africa, as founding Director of the Global Practice for Financial Inclusion and Infrastructure, and as Manager of Resource Mobilization for the Bank's concessional finance window, the International Development Association (IDA).

Over the past decade he has advised and assessed the Asian Development Bank, African Development Bank, Caribbean Development Bank, European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, European Commission, Inter-American Development Bank Group, Islamic Development Bank, and World Bank Group. With Centennial, he has served on MOPAN assessment teams and as an evaluator on the AfDB Project Completion Report Evaluation Notes. He is a Professorial Lecturer in the International Development Studies program at the Elliott School of International Affairs, George Washington University.
